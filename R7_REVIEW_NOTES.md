# Mercury Dex — R7 Review Pass

R7 removes the misleading **Pending Integration** bucket. Every one of the 118 formerly pending roster rows now has a complete NextDex-facing package: typing, stats, Primary abilities, Innates, and level-up moves.

## Status model

- **Approved roster** — recovered, source-bound, canonical, imported, or explicitly owner-locked data.
- **Review draft** — a complete proposal where one or more details were never owner-sealed. These are intentionally visible so they can be reviewed rather than left blank.

No R7 review draft is being represented as a proven ROM/source binding.
